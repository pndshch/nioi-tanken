# においたんけん / Nioi Tanken — Smell Expedition

A playful smell-journal for kids 4–8. Find smells around the house and grow a garden.

**Live**: https://pndshch.github.io/nioi-tanken/

## What it is

Most kids' apps target sight and sound. **Nioi Tanken** is for the most under-served sense: **smell**. It is the only sense the browser cannot measure for you — so the browser becomes a journal, and the world becomes the playground. The kid sniffs the kitchen, the bath, a book, a flower; taps the matching card; a colored flower blooms in the in-app garden. Sixteen smell categories. Six rooms. One tiny milestone-knowledge popup the first few times the kid logs.

## Why

- **Smell vocabulary**. Sweet, sour, woody, soapy, musty… kids learn to *name* what they smell.
- **Olfactory attention**. The today-mission ("find a sweet smell!") gives a reason to use their nose during the day.
- **Smell × memory**. Smell is the sense most tied to memory; the app gently makes that link feel real.
- **Garden as personal trace**. Every kid's garden is unique to their home and week.

## Audience

Ages 4–8. Reading-level Japanese (hiragana with `ふりがな`-friendly katakana use), and plain English. Bilingual JP/EN toggle in top-right.

## Notes

- Single static `index.html` — no libs, no build, no tracking, no accounts.
- LocalStorage-only.
- Mobile-first, but works fine on tablet/desktop.
- Quiet chime on +1, gentle bloom animation, no harsh sounds.
- Reset button at the bottom.
